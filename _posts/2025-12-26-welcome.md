This is the beginning of something new. I've been meaning to create a space where I can share my thoughts on design, productivity, and the occasional photograph for a while now.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about a few things that I find myself thinking about often:

- **Design philosophy** — how simplicity and clarity can make interfaces more human
- **Better ways to work** — systems, tools, and habits that help me stay focused
- **Photography** — moments captured and the stories behind them

## Why Now?

Sometimes the best time to start is simply when you decide to. This blog is built with Jekyll and hosted on GitHub Pages—a setup that's minimal, fast, and lets me focus on writing rather than managing infrastructure.

> The details are not the details. They make the design.
> — Charles Eames

I'm excited to see where this goes. If any of this resonates with you, feel free to reach out.
